# Under the River: Survival Story Episode

A 60-90 second second-person survival thriller: real 3D low-poly sets with flat 2D stickmen acting inside them, rules, a twist and a two-choice ending, narrated with captions and sound.

## Try it

Download the free guide from [Theo's page](https://theoxmotion.blue-hippodraco.workers.dev/r/story-video-studio/), or copy the prompt here (it's the guide's `story_video_prompt.md`). Save it as `CLAUDE.md` in an empty folder, open Claude Code there and say "you pick". The preview is the creator's own example, not a Possible-generated substitute.
