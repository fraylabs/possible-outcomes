# This Cloud Weighs 100 Elephants: Science Short

A 56-second animated science Short in the flat-vector explainer style: the claim on a title card, a chain of checked facts drawn as pictures, and big numbers turned into everyday things.

## Try it

Download the free `science-shorts-studio` skill from [Theo's page](https://theoxmotion.blue-hippodraco.workers.dev/r/science-shorts-studio/), put its `science-shorts-studio` folder (the one with SKILL.md) in `~/.claude/skills/`, open Claude Code in an empty folder and say "make a science short about how heavy a cloud is". The first run installs its free tools. The preview is the creator's own example, not a Possible-generated substitute.
