# Brief for the Animated Shorts Maker

## Story topic and the facts to cover

A short lesson: why the Tacoma Narrows Bridge twisted itself apart on 7 November 1940, four months after it opened. A steady wind of about 40 mph, not a storm, set the deck twisting until it broke. Teach the one idea behind it (the wind fed the twisting, each twist made the next push stronger, often called flutter) with an everyday analogy. The only life lost was a dog, Tubby, left in a car on the bridge. What changed after: bridges are tested in wind tunnels before they're built. Check every fact against a good source.

It's for X (and maybe YouTube Shorts). It must end on this one line on screen, exactly: "Email, logins and 2FA codes for each of your AI agents. actas.dev"
