What should it be? An ant colony toy: ants leave the nest, wander, find food the player drops, and carry it home along pheromone trails that fade over time. The player can drop food, place walls and erase.

How should it look and feel? Warm, earthy palette on a dark soil background, trails glowing faintly, smooth at 60fps, a nice starting scene with a nest and a couple of food piles on first open.

Controls and devices: Mouse and touch.
