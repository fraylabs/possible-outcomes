# He Practiced Smiling In The Mirror: Dark Story Reel

A 63-second creepy "He did X" story reel in fast greyscale sketches, written, drawn and voiced by the skill, with word-by-word captions and a sound bed made in code.

## Try it

Download the free `dark-story-reels` skill from [Theo's page](https://theoxmotion.blue-hippodraco.workers.dev/r/dark-story-reels/), put its `dark-story-reels` folder (the one with SKILL.md) in `~/.claude/skills/`, open Claude Code in an empty folder and say "make a dark story reel, you pick the story". The first run installs its free tools. The preview is the creator's own example, not a Possible-generated substitute.
