Edit talking_head.mp4 into a tighter cut.

1. Transcribe it with Whisper, with a timestamp on every word.
2. Remove every pause longer than 0.45 s.
3. Never cut inside a word. End each piece at least 0.15 s
   after its last word, measured on the audio.
4. No piece shorter than 1.2 s.
5. Export the cut as an FCPXML file for DaVinci Resolve,
   with extra footage on both sides of every cut.
6. List every cut you made and why.
7. Save every note I give you to rules.md, and read it before every edit.

---

Starter rules for rules.md (from the same guide; every number came from a real note):

Pacing
- gaps between lines: 0.45 s max
- a short beat (~0.3 s) before a spoken price
- jump cuts only at sentence ends
- no shot under 1.2 s
- never speed a voice up to hit a length

Pictures
- a shot starts on the word that names it, never after
- no whoosh transitions unless asked
- camera stays still on talking heads

Captions
- lower third, never over the face
- one idea per caption, brand names never split
- hidden while hook text is on screen

Sound
- one voice for the whole ad
- music 8 dB under every word, cut on the beat
- final mix at -14 LUFS
