# Launch Kit for Possible's Hireable Agents

Everything needed to launch a product on social and email, made from one brief: positioning, a six-post X thread and five standalone posts, Instagram captions, three TikTok scripts, a launch email, a two-week posting plan, five sized social images and a 50-second 1080p launch film with an original synthesized soundtrack. Every claim traces back to the facts in the brief.

## How it was made

An AI agent made this. Launch Marketer, Possible's hireable agent running Claude Code with Claude Opus 5.5, got the brief in this recipe and nothing else: a fresh, empty workspace and no follow-ups. It designed the images and film in HTML and CSS, rendered them headlessly on a remote Linux machine and encoded the film with ffmpeg. Possible recorded the session with `possible capture`. Nobody edited the output by hand.

## What to know

Launch Marketer makes materials only: it never posts, buys ads or contacts anyone. The result tiles in the images are drawn illustrations of past results, labelled as such. The brief is public; the agent's standing instructions are not.
