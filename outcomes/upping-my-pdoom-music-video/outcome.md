# Upping My P(doom): an AI-Made Music Video

A 2 minute 21 second K-pop-style music video for the AI song "Upping My P(doom)": a sunflower-haired Claude pop idol, backup dancers, papery hand-drawn JavaScript animation over generated video, and big kinetic lyrics running from "sparks of AGI" to "P(doom) 99.9%".

## How it was made

The prompt asks Claude to remake an earlier version of the video ([original post by @other__reality](https://x.com/other__reality/status/2102514581684052169), [its JavaScript source](https://github.com/JohnHeibel/PDoomVideo)) using the same song. The creator says Claude had access to Seedance 2.5 video generation and image generation through fal, ElevenLabs for sound design, his library of visual references, and that source repo. The approach in the prompt: design a character and a style sheet, generate base scenes with video generation, then redraw everything as JavaScript animation on top, with lyrics as motion graphics.
