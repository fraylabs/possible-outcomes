# How a Hole in Your Yard Becomes a Pond

A 60-75 second "How a hole in your yard becomes a ..." 3D Short: a house and lawn over a block of soil cut open like a cake, built step by step in Blender with a calm narrator.

## Try it

Download the free guide from [Theo's page](https://theoxmotion.blue-hippodraco.workers.dev/r/whatif-3d-studio/), or copy the prompt here (it's the guide's `whatif_guide.md`). Save it as `CLAUDE.md` in an empty folder, open Claude Code there and say "Make one video. You pick the topic.". The preview is the creator's own example, not a Possible-generated substitute.
