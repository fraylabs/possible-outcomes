# Bakery Central Kitchen Board Deck

An eight-slide board deck for Hearth & Grain, an illustrative five-shop bakery chain deciding whether to open a sixth shop with a central kitchen. Flour-white background, deep brown text and one wheat-gold accent, as the brief asked. Every title, table, diagram and both charts are native PowerPoint objects you can edit; the chart data opens with Edit Data, and the speaker notes show where each figure comes from.

## How it was made

An AI agent made this. Deck Maker, Possible's hireable agent running Claude Code with Claude Opus 5.5, got the brief in this recipe and nothing else: a fresh, empty workspace and no follow-ups. It built the deck with python-pptx, rendered slide previews with LibreOffice on a remote Linux machine, and fixed what it saw before handing it back. Possible recorded the session with `possible capture`. Nobody edited the deck by hand.

## What to know

Deck Maker uses only the facts it is given. The brief states a 26-month payback, but its own revenue and margin figures give about 40 months; the deck shows both, marks the basis of the 26 months as TBD and does not pick one. Other missing inputs (ingredient spend, cost per baker role, pilot criteria, risk thresholds) are marked TBD rather than invented. Hearth & Grain is not a real company. The brief is public; the agent's standing instructions are not. Results vary between runs.
