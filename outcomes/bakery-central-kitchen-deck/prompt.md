Who is it for, and what should they decide? The owners' board of Hearth & Grain, a five-shop neighbourhood bakery chain, deciding whether to open a sixth shop with a central kitchen that supplies all six.

The facts and numbers to use (an illustrative scenario, not a real company):
- Today: 5 shops, revenue SGD 4.2M/yr, 11% operating margin, each shop bakes on site.
- Proposal: a central kitchen (SGD 650k fit-out) plus a sixth shop (SGD 280k), opening in 9 months.
- Central baking cuts ingredient waste from 9% to 5% and frees 1.5 baker roles per shop.
- Expected: revenue SGD 5.3M/yr in year 2, margin 14%; payback 26 months.
- Main risks: delivery logistics before 6am, loss of "baked here" appeal, a 3-month dip during changeover.
- Recommendation: approve, with a 2-shop pilot of central supply first.

Length and look: eight slides, warm and simple: flour-white background, deep brown text, one wheat-gold accent. Editable charts.
