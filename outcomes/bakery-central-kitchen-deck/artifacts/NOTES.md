## Derived figures (arithmetic)
- Total investment: 650k + 280k = SGD 930k
- Operating profit today: 4.2M × 11% = SGD 462k/yr
- Operating profit in year 2: 5.3M × 14% = SGD 742k/yr
- Operating profit gain: 742k − 462k = SGD 280k/yr
- Revenue gain: 5.3M − 4.2M = SGD 1.1M/yr (+26%)
- Simple payback on the profit gain: 930k ÷ 280k = 3.32 years ≈ 40 months
- Annual benefit implied by the stated 26-month payback: 930k ÷ 26 × 12 ≈ SGD 429k/yr
- Gap between the two: 429k − 280k ≈ SGD 149k/yr
- Baker roles freed: 1.5 × 6 shops = 9, or 1.5 × 5 existing shops = 7.5

## Inconsistency flagged
The brief states a 26-month payback. Using only the brief's revenue and margin figures, payback works out to about 40 months. The deck shows both numbers and marks the basis of the 26 months as TBD (slides 2, 5, 8). It does not choose between them.

## Assumptions
- "Operating margin" means operating profit ÷ revenue for the whole chain.
- The year-2 revenue and margin cover all six shops and include the central kitchen's costs.
- The 9 months runs from board approval to the opening of the kitchen and the sixth shop together.
- The 2-shop pilot starts once the kitchen can produce. Rollout to the remaining shops follows a pilot the board has passed (go/no-go step on slide 6).
- The "What the pilot can show" column on slide 7 describes the evidence each risk would need. No pilot results exist.
- The "To bring back before rollout" list on slide 8 collects this deck's TBDs. The brief does not set these as conditions.
