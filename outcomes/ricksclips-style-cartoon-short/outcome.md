# Ricksclips-Style Cartoon Short

A 34-second vertical narrated cartoon short in the ricksclips style that's going viral: flat, bean-shaped characters with inked faces act inside a toon-shaded three.js world, every move timed to the voiceover. Every frame is rendered in code, and the music and sound effects are made in numpy. No image or video model.

## Try it

Copy the prompt into a coding agent with a shell and fill in `my_inputs`: your story, and optionally an exact script and your voiceover file. With no script it writes one; with no voiceover it gives you the script and recording tips, then waits for your audio. Once it has the audio it builds the whole short without stopping and sends a contact sheet of stills before the long render, which takes about 3-6 s a frame on a CPU. The preview is the creator's actual output, not a Possible-generated substitute.

## What to know

You need a voiceover; the creator says that and Claude are all it takes, with $0 spent on images or video. We haven't run the prompt ourselves. The style follows the ricksclips channel; this result isn't affiliated with it, and the prompt asks for an original story, characters and jokes, never a shot-for-shot copy.

**Model provenance:** Claude Opus 5.5 is reported by the creator. Possible verified the source pairing and media presentation, not an independent generation. The preview is the creator's video at 720p, copied without re-encoding. Creator media is excluded from the repository's MIT license; the original post remains linked.
