Practice desk · Fictional process v1

A good return starts with a check.

Practice three office equipment returns. Choose what to check, then decide how to record the result using the supplied rules.

Practice only — no records updated. Nothing is sent or saved.

Approved source rules · R1–R5
  • R1 — Check first. Inspect the item and check for a receipt or matching loan record before choosing a return status. Drop-off alone does not complete a return.
  • R2 — Normal return. An undamaged item with a receipt or matching record: record item ID and condition, then mark complete.
  • R3 — Missing record. No receipt and no matching record: record item ID and condition, mark pending and ask the desk lead to locate the record. Do not mark complete.
  • R4 — Damage. Visible damage: record item ID and damage, mark pending review and refer to the desk lead. R4 takes priority over R2 and R3. Do not assess fees or promise refunds.
  • R5 — Simulation. No actual records, messages, learner names or stored progress.
