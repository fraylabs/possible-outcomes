# Brief for the Procedural Film Maker

## What place should it build?

The Grand Canyon, and the big story of it told in under a minute: a flat plateau, then a river cutting down through it over about five to six million years until it is a mile deep. Show the layered red and tan rock walls, the Colorado River at the bottom, the plateau rim. The film should feel like time-lapse of millions of years: the canyon deepening as we watch.

Facts to put on screen (check them against a good source): about a mile (1.8 km) deep, about 277 miles (446 km) long, carved mostly by the Colorado River in about 5 to 6 million years, while the rocks in the walls are far older, up to nearly 2 billion years at the bottom.

## Reference photos or a mood (optional)

No photos. Warm low sunlight, deep shadows in the canyon, not grey.

## The film: length, aspect, pacing, music

45 to 60 seconds, vertical 9:16 for X and phones. The claim in big words on screen in the very first second (for example "A river did this."), and every fact also shown as words on screen, because most people watch with the sound off. Calm wind and water sounds and an epic score made in code, nothing bought. It must end on this one line on screen, exactly: "Email, logins and 2FA codes for each of your AI agents. actas.dev"
