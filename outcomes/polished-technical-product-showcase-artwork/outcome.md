# Polished Technical Product Showcase Artwork

Create a clean landscape illustration that makes an abstract technical product understandable at a glance. The Burr example turns mechanical CAD linting into one fictional machined component, one precise orange inspection plane, and a restrained set of inspection markers.

## Original request

> make a new picture for burr

## What was made

A 1672 × 941 editorial product image for a website showcase card. It presents a suspended machined housing in a warm studio scene while a translucent scan plane identifies a subtle problem area.

The image is original artwork rather than a screenshot, customer model, or claim about a real inspection result.

## Why the direction worked

- **One visual metaphor:** the part represents mechanical design; the scanning plane represents automated review.
- **One dominant subject:** the component remains readable when the artwork is reduced to a gallery-card thumbnail.
- **Product-specific materials:** brushed aluminum, precision edges, and inspection marks make the category clear without a user interface.
- **A controlled accent:** orange carries the act of detection while the rest of the palette stays quiet.
- **Editorial restraint:** negative space, soft light, and the absence of embedded copy let the surrounding website own the message.
- **Explicit exclusions:** banning UI windows, fake dimensions, generic wireframes, and cyberpunk styling prevents the result from collapsing into familiar technical-product clichés.

## How to remix it

Replace the product, hero object, material language, and inspection metaphor while retaining the compositional discipline: one subject, one meaningful action, one accent color, generous breathing room, and no unnecessary text.

For a different product, describe what it does as a physical visual event. A security tool might isolate one anomalous connection; a data product might reconcile two previously misaligned structures. Avoid illustrating the industry in general when one precise product action can carry the image.

## Verification and limits

The final image was visually checked at full size and as a small showcase card. It contains no logo, brand name, UI window, watermark, fake measurement, or customer data.

This is marketing artwork. The fictional component and inspection overlay do not demonstrate Burr's CAD accuracy, geometry handling, or manufacturability analysis.
