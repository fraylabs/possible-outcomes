What should it be? A desk phone stand that holds a phone at two viewing angles (for video calls and for reading), with a slot so the charging cable can stay plugged in.

Sizes, loads and limits: fits phones 65–80 mm wide and up to 11 mm thick, including in a case; stays put when tapping the screen; footprint under 90 × 90 mm.

How will it be made? FDM printing in PLA on a 220 mm bed, without supports, and no screws or glue.
