# Hand of God Moment Reel

A 48-second flat 2D cartoon story reel of Maradona's Hand of God, 1986: every fact checked first, comic freezes, a TV replay with an arrow, a voice-over and word-timed captions.

## Try it

Download the free `moment-reel` skill from [Theo's page](https://theoxmotion.blue-hippodraco.workers.dev/r/moment-reel/), put its `moment-reel` folder (the one with SKILL.md) in `~/.claude/skills/`, open Claude Code in an empty folder and say "make a moment reel of the Hand of God". The first run installs its free tools. The preview is the creator's own example, not a Possible-generated substitute.
