# Glass and Gold Mosaic Film

An 80-second square WebGL2 animation by Chris Riley: loose glass and gold mosaic tiles form swimming fish, flying birds and a wall that turns from day to night.

## Original creator and source

[Chris Riley (@LCSlates)](https://x.com/LCSlates) shared the [video demonstration](https://x.com/LCSlates/status/2102503027340988559) on September 22, 2026, crediting Opus 5.5. This is the creator’s model attribution, not an independently reproduced run.

The [original prompt](https://x.com/LCSlates/status/2102503028859211905) asks for one self-contained HTML animation using WebGL2 and JavaScript, without libraries or external media assets. The [posted agent readout](https://x.com/LCSlates/status/2102503030469833168) describes the animated figures, a night transformation, a spiral finale and synthesized browser sound, along with visual revisions and remaining rough edges.
