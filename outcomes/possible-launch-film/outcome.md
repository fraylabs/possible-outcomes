# Possible Launch Film

A 58-second vertical launch film for possible.sh, made entirely in code. The claim is readable on frame 1, four real results arrive one per beat with the skill that made each, then the nine skills on colour panels, the price and the closing line. Every panel lands on a bar of a 120 BPM score that was also made in code, mastered to -14 LUFS.

## How it was made

The Motion Designer skill, run in Claude Code with Claude Opus 5.5, got the brief shown here and nothing else. It planned the film as states, drew every frame with Pillow, made the music with NumPy and SciPy, and had fresh reviewers score contact sheets for three rounds. It measured brand colour, timing and loudness in the source frames and again in the encoded file. Its own checks caught a missing colour tag in the first encode, and it fixed that. Nobody edited the film by hand. After delivery we changed one word in its source, "agents" to "skills", and re-ran its own final render.

Recorded: made by the Motion Designer skill in a clean folder holding only the skill and a brief, 12 November 2026.
